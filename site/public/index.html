exomachina

A jam partner that grows with you.

We are building a creative harness that runs an open-weights model on a server you control. It keeps learning from your own work.

Your work is your property. We're building it so that your work stays yours: it is never used to train anyone else's model, and it never leaves your vault without your say.

What we're building

  • Vault: encrypted storage with post-quantum key exchange and keys that you hold.
  • Model: open weights, served from local or dedicated hardware.
  • Growth: continual post-training on your context, so the model improves the longer you play with it.
  • Language: musicians, painters and writers each get their own way of talking to the machine.
